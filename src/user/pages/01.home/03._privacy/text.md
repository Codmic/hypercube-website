## No analytics. No ad SDKs. No accounts.

Hypercube ships with no analytics, crash-reporting, or telemetry SDK of any kind — no Firebase, no Crashlytics, no Sentry. Nothing runs in the background: every network call is tied to a feature you actually tapped on, or a repaint of a face that's currently on screen. Turn a feature off and Hypercube simply stops using the permission it was granted for — nothing lingers.

The only outbound network calls the app makes at all are three named, feature-scoped APIs — weather, crypto prices, and currency conversion — plus web search, entirely under your control. Read the full breakdown on the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) page.
