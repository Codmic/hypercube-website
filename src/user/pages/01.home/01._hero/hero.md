# Your home screen is a cube.

Not another grid of app icons. Hypercube renders your apps on the six faces of a rotatable 3D cube — drag, fling, and snap between them — with a long-press hologram menu, folders, widgets, and 72 real-time procedural backgrounds running underneath it all.
